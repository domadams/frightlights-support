Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Do I need Philips Hue lights to use FrightLights?

No. All looping horror moods, jump-scare sound effects, and Spooky Studio timelines play full audio on your iPhone, iPad, or connected Bluetooth speaker even without smart lights connected.

2. How do I connect my Philips Hue Bridge?

  1. Make sure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your Philips Hue Bridge.
  2. Open the Lights tab inside FrightLights and tap Connect Hue Bridge.
  3. Press the physical link button on top of your Philips Hue Bridge when prompted.
  4. Select the rooms, zones, Entertainment areas, or individual bulbs you want FrightLights to control.

3. How do I restore my FrightLights Pro purchase on a new device?

  1. Open FrightLights and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Under the FrightLights Pro card at the top, tap Restore Purchases (or tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the FrightLights Pro upgrade sheet).
  3. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID or Google Play account used for the original purchase.

4. How do I manage or cancel my Halloween Season Pass subscription?

Subscriptions are managed directly by Apple or Google. On iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app → tap your name at the top → tap Subscriptions → select FrightLights.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

FrightLights ("the App") is developed by Dominic Adams ("we", "us", or "our"). We built FrightLights with a strict privacy-first architecture: the App does not require you to create an account, does not include third-party advertising SDKs, and does not sell or rent your personal data.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

A. Local Network & Philips Hue Integration: When you pair a Philips Hue Bridge, the App communicates directly with your Hue Bridge over your local Wi-Fi network. Your Hue Bridge IP address, application key, and selected light/room preferences are stored exclusively on your device using encrypted system storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) and local app storage. We do not transmit your Hue Bridge credentials, room names, or light configurations to any external server.

B. Optional Ring Doorbell Integration: If you choose to connect a Ring Doorbell in Settings, the App authenticates directly from your device with Ring's official OAuth service. Your session token is stored locally in your device's secure keychain and is never sent to our servers. You can disconnect your Ring account at any time in the App's Settings tab, which immediately deletes the stored token from your device.

C. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (RevenueCat): FrightLights uses Apple StoreKit, Google Play Billing, and RevenueCat to process and validate FrightLights Pro purchases (Halloween Season Pass and Lifetime Haunt Pass) and to support cross-device purchase restoration. Payment transactions are processed directly by Apple or Google; we never see or store your credit card or billing details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and store purchase receipt metadata solely to verify your Pro entitlement status. View RevenueCat's Privacy Policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

D. Privacy-Scrubbed Crash Diagnostics (Sentry): To keep the App reliable, FrightLights uses Sentry to report unexpected application crashes and runtime errors. Crash reports are automatically scrubbed on-device before transmission to remove local IP addresses, Hue Bridge identifiers, room/light/sensor names, and authentication tokens. No screenshots, session replays, or personal identifiers are collected.

2. Data Sharing & Third-Party Tracking

  • No Tracking: FrightLights does not track you across third-party apps or websites.
  • No Data Sales: We never sell, rent, or trade user data with third parties or data brokers.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your smart-home credentials and custom Spooky Studio shows are stored locally on your device, uninstalling the App or disconnecting your integrations in the Settings tab permanently removes that data from your device. To request deletion of anonymous purchase receipt records associated with your device, contact us at domadams2000@hotmail.com.

4. Children's Privacy

FrightLights does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
